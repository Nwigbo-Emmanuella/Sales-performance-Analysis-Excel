## Sales Performance Analysis (2023–2025)

## Project Overview

This project analyses sales performance data to identify trends, evaluate product and regional performance, understand customer purchasing patterns, and assess the impact of promotions.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Excel Charts and Data Visualization
* Slicers and Interactive Dashboard
* Data Analysis and Business Reporting

## Project Objectives

* Analyse sales performance over time.
* Compare sales across regions and product categories.
* Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour.
* Examine promotional performance.
* Present key performance indicators in an interactive dashboard.

## Key Areas of Analysis

* Sales Trends: Understand how sales change over time.
* Regional Performance: Compare sales across geographical regions.
* Product Performance: Identify products and categories contributing to sales.
* Customer Analysis: Explore purchasing patterns across customer segments.
* Promotional Analysis: Assess sales performance associated with promotions.

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to organise data, analyse business performance, and communicate findings through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis can help stakeholders identify performance gaps, investigate sales trends, and make more informed decisions about products, regions, customers, and promotions.

## Key Insights

* Regional Performance: The North region recorded the highest sales among the regions shown in the dashboard.
* Product Performance: Printers were the highest-selling product category by sales value in the dashboard.
* Sales Monitoring: The dashboard summarizes sales performance using key metrics, including total sales, quantity sold, shipping costs, discounts, and orders.
* Customer Analysis: The dashboard compares sales across customer types to support customer-segment analysis.
* Promotional Analysis: The promotion chart provides a basis for comparing sales contributions across promotional categories.

 ## Business Recommendations

* Regional Strategy: Investigate the factors driving sales in the North region and use those findings to guide regional marketing and inventory planning.
* Inventory Planning: Review printer demand and stock availability to reduce the risk of stockouts while avoiding unnecessary overstocking.
* Promotion Evaluation: Compare promotional sales with costs and profitability before increasing campaign spending.
* Customer Strategy: Use customer-type performance to tailor marketing and retention initiatives.
* Ongoing Performance Tracking: Monitor sales trends, regional performance, and product contributions regularly to identify changes and support informed business decisions.
